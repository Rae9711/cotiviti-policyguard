Version: Calendar Year 2026 Demo Excerpt
Section: XI.D — Demo Procedure Reporting Guidance

1. General principles
When reporting evaluation and management services together with other
procedures on the same date of service, documentation must support medical
necessity for each reported service.

2. Demo procedure EXAMPLE1
Procedure code EXAMPLE1 may be reported when the clinical documentation
supports the described service. Effective for dates of service on or after

policy review before payment adjudication. (MACHINE-ACTIONABLE DEMO CHANGE)

3. Demo procedure EXAMPLE2

procedural service is documented.

4. Documentation expectations
Providers should retain supporting notes sufficient to substantiate

6. Effective period
